# References: Advanced Hydroponic Systems and Maintenance

1. [Pump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pump) - Wikipedia - Covers pump types including centrifugal, submersible, and peristaltic pumps; explains head pressure, flow rate, and efficiency curves relevant to selecting and sizing pumps for recirculating hydroponic systems.

2. [Biofilm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biofilm) - Wikipedia - Explains how bacterial and algal biofilms form on reservoir walls, tubing, and net pots; details the microbial succession and why biofilm removal between crop cycles is essential for food safety and system performance.

3. [Redundancy (engineering)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redundancy_(engineering)) - Wikipedia - Engineering principles of backup systems and fault tolerance directly applicable to hydroponic pump redundancy, dual reservoir designs, and alarm systems that prevent crop loss from component failure.

4. Hydroponic Food Production (7th ed.) - Howard M. Resh - CRC Press - Covers reservoir sizing formulas, plant spacing tables by crop type, pump maintenance schedules, and the sanitation protocols used between crops in commercial recirculating systems.

5. Hydroponics: A Practical Guide for the Soilless Grower - J. Benton Jones Jr. - CRC Press - Practical guidance on tubing selection, fitting types, system leak prevention, and the step-by-step cleaning procedures that prevent pathogen carryover between crop cycles.

6. [USDA Good Agricultural Practices](https://www.ams.usda.gov/services/auditing/gap-ghp) - USDA AMS - Official GAP audit standards for hydroponic operations, including water quality testing, sanitation documentation, and traceability requirements relevant to system maintenance between cycles.

7. [EPA: Recirculating Water Systems](https://www.epa.gov/agriculture/agriculture-water) - EPA - Guidelines on managing recirculating agricultural water systems to minimize pathogen risk, water waste, and nutrient discharge — directly applicable to run-to-waste versus recirculating system decisions.

8. [Real Python: Scheduling in Python](https://realpython.com/python-schedule/) - Real Python - Tutorial on automating timed tasks in Python — applicable to writing pump scheduling code and maintenance reminder systems for hydroponic automation projects.

9. [MIT OCW: Systems Engineering](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/16-842-fundamentals-of-systems-engineering-fall-2015/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - Systems engineering methodology covering failure mode analysis, redundancy design, and reliability modeling — provides analytical tools for designing fault-tolerant hydroponic systems.

10. [Khan Academy: Work and Energy](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-physics-1/ap-work-and-energy) - Khan Academy - Physics of work and energy relevant to understanding pump power consumption, pipe friction losses, and efficiency calculations for sizing submersible pumps in hydroponic reservoirs.
