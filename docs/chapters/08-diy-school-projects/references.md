# References: DIY Systems and School Projects

1. [Mason jar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mason_jar) - Wikipedia - History and specifications of the sealed glass jar used in Kratky passive hydroponic systems; the standard mason jar's thread size and volume make it ideal for single-plant nutrient solution reservoirs.

2. [Microgreens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microgreens) - Wikipedia - Covers the cultivation of young seedlings harvested at the cotyledon stage; microgreens are among the fastest and most cost-effective crops for beginner hydroponic projects in schools.

3. [Science fair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fair) - Wikipedia - Overview of science fair project structure including hypothesis formation, experimental design, data collection, and presentation requirements — directly applicable to designing hydroponic experiments for school competitions.

4. Hydroponics: A Practical Guide for the Soilless Grower - J. Benton Jones Jr. - CRC Press - Includes beginner-friendly system builds and crop selection guidance, with detailed instructions for converting household containers into functional Kratky and DWC setups.

5. Hydroponic Food Production (7th ed.) - Howard M. Resh - CRC Press - Chapter on small-scale and educational systems provides verified build specifications for PVC pipe NFT and storage-tote DWC systems with materials lists and step-by-step assembly.

6. [USDA: School Garden Resources](https://www.usda.gov/media/blog/2015/04/09/usda-supports-school-gardens) - USDA - Federal resources supporting school food gardens, including safety guidelines, curriculum integration strategies, and grant information for K-12 hydroponic and garden programs.

7. [EPA: Safe Use of Fertilizers in Schools](https://www.epa.gov/safepestcontrol/schools-and-pesticides) - EPA - Guidance on the safe handling and storage of agricultural chemicals (including hydroponic nutrients) in school settings, covering labeling requirements, ventilation, and student safety protocols.

8. [Khan Academy: Experimental Design](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability/designing-studies/experiments-stats-library/a/introduction-to-experiment-design) - Khan Academy - Covers hypothesis testing, control variables, data collection planning, and statistical thinking for designing valid science fair experiments with hydroponic systems.

9. [MIT OCW: Design and Manufacturing](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-007-design-and-manufacturing-i-spring-2009/) - MIT OpenCourseWare - Introductory engineering design course with hands-on project methodology applicable to planning, building, and iterating on DIY hydroponic systems under budget constraints.

10. [NASA: Growing Food in Schools](https://www.nasa.gov/audience/foreducators/plantgrowth/reference/Hydro_Intro.html) - NASA - NASA educational resources on hydroponics for K-12 classrooms, including simple Kratky and wick system instructions aligned to STEM curriculum standards.
