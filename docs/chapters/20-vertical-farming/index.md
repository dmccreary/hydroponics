# Vertical Farming and Commercial Operations

## Summary

This chapter examines the commercial endpoint of the course: multi-tier growing rack systems, tower gardens, container farms, and rooftop farms; profiles of industry leaders (AeroFarms, Bowery Farming, Gotham Greens); HVAC requirements and yield per square meter at commercial scale; the roles of robotic harvesting and AI crop management in reducing labor costs; and the supply chain and regulatory dimensions — food miles, cold chain logistics, organic certification, and Good Agricultural Practices — that govern bringing hydroponic produce to market.
