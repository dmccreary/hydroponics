# References: Solar Energy and Power Systems

1. [Photovoltaics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photovoltaics) - Wikipedia - Comprehensive coverage of photovoltaic technology including solar cell physics, panel efficiency metrics, system components (panels, charge controllers, batteries, inverters), and the economics of solar power generation.

2. [Lithium iron phosphate battery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithium_iron_phosphate_battery) - Wikipedia - Covers LiFePO4 battery chemistry, cycle life, thermal stability advantages over lithium-ion, depth-of-discharge ratings, and why this chemistry is preferred for off-grid hydroponic solar storage.

3. [Maximum power point tracking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_power_point_tracking) - Wikipedia - Explains MPPT charge controller algorithms that maximize power harvest from solar panels across varying irradiance and temperature conditions, typically delivering 10–30% more energy than PWM controllers.

4. Photovoltaic Systems Engineering (4th ed.) - Roger A. Messenger & Amir Abtahi - CRC Press - Authoritative textbook on solar PV system design covering panel selection, sizing calculations, charge controller types, battery bank design, inverter selection, and economic analysis including LCOE.

5. Renewable Energy: Power for a Sustainable Future - Godfrey Boyle - Oxford University Press - Broad renewable energy resource covering photovoltaic principles, solar resource assessment, system sizing methodology, and grid-tied versus off-grid design relevant to powering hydroponic operations.

6. [NREL: PVWatts Calculator](https://pvwatts.nrel.gov/) - National Renewable Energy Laboratory - Online tool from NREL for estimating annual solar energy production based on location, panel size, tilt angle, and system losses; essential for sizing solar power systems for hydroponic operations.

7. [NREL: Solar Resource Data](https://www.nrel.gov/gis/solar-resource-maps.html) - National Renewable Energy Laboratory - Geographic solar irradiance maps and peak sun hour data for the United States, needed to calculate panel sizing and annual energy yield for hydroponic farm power systems.

8. [NASA: Solar Energy Resources](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/) - NASA POWER - NASA's global solar and meteorological data portal providing location-specific solar irradiance, temperature, and humidity data for any point on Earth — invaluable for off-grid hydroponic system sizing.

9. [Real Python: Data Visualization for Energy](https://realpython.com/python-matplotlib-guide/) - Real Python - Matplotlib tutorial applicable to building energy monitoring dashboards that visualize solar panel output, battery state of charge, and hydroponic system power consumption over time.

10. [MicroPython: INA219 Power Monitor](https://docs.micropython.org/en/latest/library/index.html) - MicroPython.org - MicroPython library reference for I2C devices including INA219 current and voltage monitoring IC used to measure solar panel output, battery charge current, and load power in hydroponic solar systems.
